## **CASE 1\. Tokenization of Real-World Assets (RWA)**

### **1\. Context**

The world of finance and ownership is gradually moving into digital form.  
However, real assets \- real estate, commodities, bonds, and energy \- remain:

* hard to access for a wide range of people  
* difficult to manage and transfer  
* non-transparent and not always liquid

Tokenization makes it possible to turn real assets into digital tokens and open global access to them.

### **2\. Problem Area**

Within this track, participants may address one or more of the following problems:

* High barrier to entry (for example, buying real estate or bonds requires large amounts of capital)  
* Low liquidity of real-world assets  
* Lack of transparency in ownership and transactions  
* Complex processes for accounting and income distribution (rent, dividends, etc.)  
* Lack of global access (local markets, limited audience)  
* Distrust of intermediaries

***NOTE: You do not have to solve all of these problems \- choose a focus and develop a solution.***

### **3\. Task**

Create a product on Solana that enables one of the following:

* tokenize real-world assets (for example: real estate, bonds, commodities, or energy) with fractional ownership and subsequent trading  
*   
* manage ownership, transfer, or yield via blockchain  
* simplify access to investment or asset management  
* The solution must demonstrate a complete user journey (end-to-end flow), including issuance, ownership, and interaction with the asset.

This can be either infrastructure (a protocol) or an end product (a platform / application).

### **4\. Possible Solution Formats**

You may build:

* A platform for fractional real estate ownership  
* An energy tokenization system (for example, P2P electricity trading)  
* Tokenized bonds or financial instruments  
* An asset tokenization platform (a universal builder)  
* Solutions for ownership rights accounting and income distribution  
* A marketplace for trading tokenized assets  
* Infrastructure (protocols, SDKs, developer tools)  
* solutions that ensure the link between a physical asset and its digital representation (oracle, proof-of-asset)

***NOTE: You may go beyond these examples if your solution fits the theme.***

### **5\. Minimum Requirements (MVP)**

Your solution must:

* use the Solana blockchain  
* include a deployed smart contract (program)  
* allow users to:  
  * create / issue tokens  
  * manage ownership or transactions  
  * have a basic interface (web / bot / demo)  
  * provide a link between the real asset and the token (for example: oracle, off-chain data, or another verification mechanism)

### **6\. Constraints**

❌ You may not submit only an idea or a presentation without a prototype  
❌ You may not rely only on off-chain logic without using Solana  
❌ You may not build “pseudo-Web3” solutions without real token operations

### **7\. What Is Important to Show**

In the presentation and demo:

* what problem you are solving  
* how your product works (user flow)  
* how Solana is used (not formally, but in substance)  
* the solution architecture (briefly)  
* a demo or working prototype  
* scalability potential

## **CASE 2\. AI \+ Blockchain: Autonomous Smart Contracts**

### **1\. Context**

Blockchain provides transparency and immutability, but smart contracts remain rigidly programmed:

* they do not adapt to changes  
* they do not make complex decisions  
* they do not work with unstructured data

On the other hand, AI can analyze data, learn, and make decisions,  
but it does not inherently provide transparency and trust.

Combining AI and blockchain opens the possibility of creating autonomous systems that:

* make decisions  
* manage assets  
* act according to predefined logic  
* while remaining verifiable on-chain

### **2\. Problem Area**

You may work on one or more of the following problems:

* Smart contracts do not adapt to changing conditions (market conditions, user behavior)  
* Solutions require manual management or centralized control  
* There is no transparent way to use AI in financial or decentralized systems  
* It is difficult to trust AI if its decisions are not transparent  
* There are no tools for automatic management of DeFi / assets / processes

Your task is to show how AI can become part of on-chain logic.

### **3\. Hackathon Task**

Create a product on Solana in which:

* AI takes part in decision-making  
* these decisions lead to a change in the smart contract state (on-chain actions)  
*   
* the system operates semi-autonomously or fully autonomously

This may be:

* an AI agent  
* an autonomous protocol  
* an asset management system  
* infrastructure for AI \+ Web3

### **4\. Possible Solution Formats**

You may build:

* an AI agent that manages assets (trading, allocation, risk management)  
* a DeFi protocol with dynamic parameters (rates, fees, limits)  
* a DAO with an AI assistant for decision-making  
* a data analysis system with automatic on-chain actions  
* autonomous services (for example, insurance, lending, income distribution)  
* infrastructure for integrating AI with smart contracts

You may go beyond these examples \- the key point is that AI must genuinely influence the system logic.

### **5\. Minimum Requirements (MVP)**

Your solution must:

* use the Solana blockchain  
* include a deployed smart contract (program)  
* include an AI component (model / API / agent)  
* demonstrate the following chain:  
* AI → decision → on-chain transaction → smart contract state change  
* have a demo (web / bot / usage scenario)

### **6\. Constraints**

❌ You may not do “AI for checkbox purposes” without impact on the logic  
❌ You may not build off-chain AI only, without integration with the contract  
❌ You may not submit a static contract without adaptability  
❌ You may not limit the solution to AI recommendations without actual on-chain execution

Simplified models (API, mock) are allowed if the autonomy logic is demonstrated.

### **7\. What Is Important to Show**

In the presentation and demo:

* what task the system solves  
* how AI makes decisions  
* how these decisions affect the smart contract  
* the level of the system's autonomy  
* the architecture (AI \+ blockchain connection)  
* how AI initiates or influences on-chain transactions  
* submit your solution on colosseum.com (submissions open on April 7, 2026\)
